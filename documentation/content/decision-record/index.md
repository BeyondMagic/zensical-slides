Legend:

- **Planned**: template of decision record, no user made changes;
- **Proposed**: the decision is under discussion and has not been finalized yet;
- **Rejected**: the decision was considered but ultimately not chosen;
- **Accepted**: the decision has been made and is being implemented;
- **Deprecated**: the decision was once accepted but is no longer in use or recommended.
- **Architecture**: decision for database, files organization, folder organization, code, references organization, AI usage;
- **Social**: decision for guides for human interactions, team agreements, collaboration rules, or cultural choices within and across teams to build transparency;
- **Design**: decision for process or style of user interface and user experience, kind of behaviour (intuitive or new paradigm), or specific tooling;
- **Product**: decision for scope, target userbase, feature priorization, or roadmap pivots;
- **Security**: decision for  compliance choices, risk acceptance criterea, data retention policies, or security-related trade-offs;
- **Operational**: decision for  choices for deployment pipelines, hosting strategies, incident response protocols, or operational tooling;
- **Strategy**: decision for  high-level business models, competitive analysis of alternatives, budget allocations, team-splitting choices, vendor/procurement strategies.
